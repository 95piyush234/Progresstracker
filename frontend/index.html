How To Use Progress Tracker Pro

A visual walkthrough for new users

Understand what the website is for, how to log progress, and where to see your graphs, history, proof uploads, and backups.

What is Progress Tracker Pro?

Your personal progress workspace for any goal.

Progress Tracker Pro is a powerful yet simple tool to track any goal or milestone you care about. Whether you're studying for an exam, training for fitness, gaming climbs, reading books, building habits, or managing projects—this app keeps everything organized in one beautiful, focused workspace. Track progress, upload proof images, analyze trends, and maintain accountability all in one place.

Create Custom Trackers Log Progress Daily Upload Proof Images View Analytics Backup Your Data
1. Dashboard

See your whole workspace at a glance

Start on Dashboard to check total trackers, today’s progress, due milestones, overall completion, recent movement, and the strongest category in your workspace.

2. Create Tracker

Build any custom tracker you want

Use Create Tracker to define a title, category, unit, target, deadline, color, icon, notes, creator name, and custom fields that match your goal.

3. Quick Log + Proof

Open a tracker, add progress, then attach proof

The detail drawer lets you update amount, date, tag, and note. You can also upload a screenshot or photo as proof, then save it so it appears in Timeline and History.

4. Graph View

Use the Graph page for visual progress insights

Open Graph to compare trackers, review weekly movement, read category performance, and understand your progress with clearer visual boards.

5. History

Review all your entries

History shows every single log entry across all trackers in chronological order: • Filter by Tracker: Focus on one goal or view everything • Search: Find specific entries by keyword • View Details: Click any entry to see the full details, proof image, and notes • Edit or Delete: Correct mistakes or remove entries • Proof Images: All uploaded images are visible here This is your complete audit trail of all progress logged.

6. Managing Trackers

Edit, archive, or delete trackers

For any tracker, you have several management options:

  • Edit: Open the detail drawer and click the edit button to change tracker settings.
  • Archive: Archive completed trackers to clean up your workspace (they're still visible).
  • Reset Progress: Clear all logs and start fresh while keeping tracker settings.
  • Delete: Permanently remove a tracker and all its logs (be careful!).
  • Sort & Filter: Organize by category, priority, or status on the Trackers page.
7. Backup & Sync

Keep your data safe and accessible

Your workspace has two storage options:

  • Local Storage: All data is saved on your device. You can use the app fully offline.
  • Create an Account: Sign up with email to enable cloud sync and access from multiple devices.
  • Export Backup: Go to History and export your entire workspace as a JSON file.
  • Import Backup: Restore a previously exported workspace to recover your data.
  • Cloud Sync: When signed in, your data syncs automatically to the backend.
  • Mobile Access: After creating an account, access your data from mobile or web.
8. Account & Authentication

Secure access and data sync

Progress Tracker Pro uses email-based authentication for security:

  • Create Access: Enter your name, email, and password. You'll receive an OTP (one-time password) to verify.
  • Sign In: Use your email and password. Forgot your password? Use the password reset link.
  • OTP Verification: After entering your credentials, check your email for a 6-digit code. Enter it within 10 minutes.
  • Session Management: Your session stays active so you can use the app continuously.
  • Logout: Click your account name and select Logout. Your local data remains intact.
  • Privacy: Your data is encrypted and stored securely. Only you can access it.
9. Priority & Status

Organize your goals

Each tracker has a priority level and status:

  • Priority Levels: Critical, High, Medium, Low — helps you focus on what matters most.
  • Status: Active (working on it), Completed (finished), or Archived (done but keep visible).
  • Deadline: Set an end date so you stay on track.
  • Filter by Priority: On Trackers page, show only Critical or High priority items.
  • Quick Filter: Use the toolbar to show Active, Completed, or Archived trackers separately.
10. Tips & Tricks

Get the most out of Progress Tracker Pro

  • Use Custom Fields: Add extra columns to trackers (e.g., mood, location, difficulty).
  • Log Daily: The more consistent your logs, the more valuable your data and insights.
  • Upload Proof Early: Attach screenshots/photos while they're fresh, not days later.
  • Set Realistic Targets: Choose goals you can actually achieve to maintain motivation.
  • Review Weekly: Check Graphs on Fridays to see your progress and plan the next week.
  • Use Tags: Tag entries (Struggle, Breakthrough, Routine) to categorize types of logs.
  • Create Template Trackers: Use similar trackers for recurring goals (Weekly Study, Daily Runs).
  • Mobile First: Check your workspace on mobile for quick logging during the day.
  • Export Regularly: Create monthly backups so you never lose your data.
  • Celebrate Wins: Take screenshots of completed trackers or milestones you achieve.
11. Your First Steps

Follow this recommended path

  1. 1. Explore the Dashboard: Get familiar with how your progress is displayed.
  2. 2. Click a Sample Tracker: Open one of the 3 pre-loaded trackers to see the detail view.
  3. 3. Try Logging Progress: Add a test log entry to a sample tracker (you can delete it later).
  4. 4. Check the Graphs: See how your test entry appears in the visual charts.
  5. 5. Create Your First Goal: Click "Create Tracker" and build your first custom tracker.
  6. 6. Add Real Logs: Start logging your actual progress for the next few days.
  7. 7. Upload a Proof Image: Add a screenshot or photo to one of your entries.
  8. 8. Create an Account: Sign up for cloud sync so your data is backed up and accessible everywhere.
  9. 9. Explore Each View: Spend time in Dashboard, Graphs, and History to understand the full picture.
  10. 10. Build Your Routine: Check your dashboard daily and log progress consistently.
